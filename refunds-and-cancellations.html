Refunds & Cancellations

All payments are processed in INR. Refund requests can be raised through the Contact Us page.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be approved in the following cases after verification:

  • Duplicate payment for the same service/order.
  • Payment deducted but the service/order was not created.
  • Service unavailable after successful payment.

Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds may not be available in the following cases:

  • Successfully delivered or processed digital service.
  • Incorrect details submitted by the user.
  • User changes mind after service processing starts.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are usually processed within 5-7 business days, or as per the payment provider and bank timeline.

Cancellation Policy

Users can cancel a service request only before service processing starts. Once processing has started, cancellation may not be available.

How To Request Support

To request a refund, cancellation, or payment support, please visit the Contact Us page and share your order/payment details.
